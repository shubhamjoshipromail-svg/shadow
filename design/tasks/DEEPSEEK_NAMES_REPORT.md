# Name availability check — report

Task: `design/tasks/DEEPSEEK_NAMES.md`. Research only. **Web access: yes.** I used web search/fetch for
products and trademarks, and `whois` from this machine for domains. Nothing was committed or pushed;
this is the only file written.

**Method and limits.** Domain checks are registry `whois` results (authoritative for "registered", not a
price). Trademark data is from public aggregators (TrademarkElite, Trademarkia, Furm, Aliyun) because
USPTO TSDR/Justia and the UK IPO blocked automated queries; class 9/42 was checked where a record was
findable, and other classes are noted. This is **not a legal clearance search** — a live class-42 (SaaS)
search was not runnable here, so anything below is a flag, not a verdict.

## Headline

All five names are compromised. All 20 domain variants I checked (`<name>.ai`, `get<name>.com`,
`<name>app.com`, `try<name>.com`) are already registered — every cell, no exceptions. Every candidate
also has at least one class-9 US trademark hit and at least one shipped AI product. The choice is
therefore about which collision is least damaging to *this* product in finance/AP.

## Details behind the table

**Tacit.** The word is the product's thesis: Polanyi's [*tacit knowledge*](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/psychology/tacit-knowledge) is precisely what the AI can't
read off the screen and must be elicited. That is rare semantic ownership. The problem is that
[Tacit.io](https://getlatka.com/companies/tacit.io/vs/tamplo.com) already sells "capture, preserve and
scale tacit knowledge" with AI, in an adjacent (industrial workforce) market; [Tacit Analytics](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/1084511-44)
and Tacit Legal's Tilder exist too. The findable marks are not direct SaaS blockers: the allowed US
Class 9 TACIT covers wearable/silent-speech hardware, the EU mark is Class 10 medical, and the only
Class 42 hit (China) is dead. Counterweight: "tacit" is a common adjective and "tacit knowledge" is a
generic academic term, so the mark is suggestiveness-weak — hard to register broadly and hard to stop
others from using. Net: best name here, but do a real class-42 clearance against Tacit.io before
committing, and use a distinctive wordmark/lockup.

**Understudy.** Semantically the neatest fit for the pixel intern, and it implies the whole arc
(apprentice → stand-in → lead teacher). But two direct hits: Understudy Labs, the YC-backed
[self-optimizing inference cloud](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SPb-understudy-the-self-optimizing-inference-cloud)
(now Orchestra), still trading at [understudylabs.com](https://understudylabs.com); and a legal-tech
project [Understudy](https://vibecode.law/showcase/understudy-612600) that is the same idea in another
vertical — "an AI-powered companion that helps junior lawyers learn from partners … transfers a
partner's tacit judgement." There is also a live, incontestable US Class 9 registration (Reg Grant,
reg. 6148456) and theatre connotes "second best, never the lead."

**Margin.** The weakest of the three better options for this product. [Margin](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/margin)
is a YC S23 liquor-retail POS (usemargin.com) with a live Class 9 application; [MRGN](https://mrgn.ai/news/)
sells AI for SMB financial decisions; a separate [Margin](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/535707-10)
appears in software databases. None of these is an AI-apprentice product, so the AI collision is the
lightest of the five. The fatal issue is the vertical: in AP/finance, "margin" is a core metric and
"margin call" is a stress event — buyers and search engines will constantly misread it.

**Mira.** Every axis is crowded. Meltwater ships an enterprise [Mira AI assistant](https://www.meltwater.com/en/ai)
to 27,000+ organisations and holds a live [US Class 9 MIRA application](https://www.trademarkelite.com/trademark/trademark-detail/98899973/MIRA);
[Mira glasses](https://trymira.com/pages/careers) uses trymira.com; [Mira Network](https://messari.io/project/mira-network/profile)
is an AI/crypto network; [Kimi Mira](https://www.kimi.com/resources/kimi-mira-introduction) is a Feishu
AI colleague; Mira Care is a fertility brand; Mira Murati is a famous AI founder. As a product name it
has no distinctiveness left. As a *character* it is still the best option (below).

**Shadow.** The baseline should go. "Shadow AI" is the established term for employees using
unsanctioned AI tools — see [Harmonic Security's 22.4M-prompt analysis](https://www.harmonic.security/resources/what-22-million-enterprise-ai-prompts-reveal-about-shadow-ai-in-2025)
and [Gartner's GenAI blind-spot guidance](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-11-19-gartner-identifies-critical-genai-blind-spots-that-cios-must-urgently-address0).
It is also already an AI product ([Taper Labs' Shadow](https://www.shadow.do/blog/shadow-1-0-your-second-brain))
and a gaming brand ([Blade](https://www.bpifrance.fr/nos-actualites/shadow-la-pepite-francaise-du-jeu-video-a-la-demande),
who filed [SHADOW 88362452](https://furm.com/trademarks/shadow-88362452) across classes 9/28/35/38/42,
since abandoned). An AI apprentice named Shadow in a finance department will read as the exact
governance problem it is meant to solve.

## Character names

The companion (round glasses, notebook: apprentice who becomes teacher) should stay human and warm.
Ranked; one line each.

| # | Name | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Mira** | Keep it as the character: means "wonder/look" (Latin *mirari*), short and international — and recommending it as the character is exactly why it must not be the product |
| 2 | **Nell** | Understated, Dickensian apprentice warmth; from Eleanor, "light" — the one who grows bright enough to teach |
| 3 | **Juno** | The advisor (Latin *monere*, "to counsel"); fits the teach-back/debrief role without being literal like "Sage" |
| 4 | **Wren** | Small, watchful, unobtrusive — the quiet observer who is always taking notes |
| 5 | **Tilly** | Friendly, unthreatening, grows into mastery (Matilda, "mighty in battle"); reads like a real colleague |
| 6 | **Oona** | Short, soft, uncommon; "one/unity" — distinctive without being twee |

**Recommended pairing: product *Tacit*, character *Mira*.** One line each: *Tacit* names the exact thing
the apprentice captures and is the only candidate with a defensible concept, while *Mira* is too
crowded to be the product but remains the warmest, most fitting name for the intern herself.
Runner-up pairing: *Understudy* + *Mira*, if the team wants the product named after the companion — but
only after checking the YC inference company and the legal-tech Understudy.

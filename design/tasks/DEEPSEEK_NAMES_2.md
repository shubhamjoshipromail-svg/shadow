# Task: name search, round 2 (research only)

Owner: DeepSeek (headless). Reviewer: Claude. Only write `design/tasks/DEEPSEEK_NAMES_2_REPORT.md`.

Read `design/tasks/DEEPSEEK_NAMES_REPORT.md` first (round 1: Tacit, Margin, Understudy, Mira, Shadow all collide).

Product: an AI apprentice that sits beside an expert at work, predicts their decisions, asks one question at a natural
pause when it can't explain one, turns the answer into tested rules, and then tutors new hires in the expert's words.
Enterprise (finance first), so it must sound trustworthy, calm and adult, not cute, not "AI-hype". The companion is a
pixel-art intern character with round glasses and a notebook (working character name: Mira) who learns, then teaches.
Themes worth mining: apprenticeship, guilds, journeyman, marginalia, notebooks, tacit/unwritten knowledge, succession,
handover, craft, oral tradition, "learns what isn't written down".

1. Generate at least 40 candidate product names: real words, coined words and two-word names. Short (≤ 10 letters
   preferred), easy to say aloud in English and German, no negative meaning in finance/HR/security.
2. Cut to the best 15 on fit, then check each (cite sources): AI/enterprise products already using it; obvious US/EU
   software trademark (class 9/42) hits; `whois` for `<name>.com` and `<name>.ai` and one natural variant
   (`get<name>.com` or `<name>hq.com`). Prefer names where at least one clean, credible domain is free.
3. Rank the top 6 with one line each on why, and the main risk. Also propose 3 tagline options for the top pick
   (short, concrete, no "AI-powered", no "unlock", no "revolutionize").

Be factual: if a check was impossible, say so; do not invent results.

# Nordwerk Maschinenbau GmbH — Accounts Payable Work Instruction
*Document AP-WI-03, version 2.1, last revised March 2019*

## 1. Purpose
This instruction describes how incoming supplier invoices are checked, coded and posted.

## 2. Checking an invoice
1. Open the invoice from the AP inbox.
2. Check that supplier name, invoice number, date and amounts are legible and complete.
3. If a purchase order exists, compare the invoice with the purchase order.

## 3. Coding
- Services, repairs and maintenance: cost center **4711** (Opex – Maintenance).
- Consumables and small parts: cost center **4720** (Opex – Consumables).
- Equipment and IT hardware: cost center **4711**, unless it is a major investment approved by management, which is coded to capex.
- Tax code: **V19** (19% German VAT) for standard invoices.
- Payment date: the due date of the invoice.

## 4. Posting
When the invoice is checked and coded, click **Post**. Unclear cases may be discussed with the team lead.
